# Contributing to PostShor

Thank you for helping the world prepare for post-quantum cryptography. Each repository may have its own, more detailed guide; this page applies when it does not.

- **Scanner and rules:** see [postshor/postshor CONTRIBUTING.md](https://github.com/postshor/postshor/blob/main/CONTRIBUTING.md). New detection rules with tests are the most valuable contribution.
- **Research and data:** corrections and reproductions are welcome. Open an issue in [postshor/research](https://github.com/postshor/research) with the run, the domain or row, and what you observed. To have a domain excluded from future measurements, open an issue there.
- **GitHub Action:** issues and feature requests go to [postshor/postshor/issues](https://github.com/postshor/postshor/issues).

All contributions are accepted under the license of the repository they are made to. Please sign off your commits (`git commit -s`) to certify the [Developer Certificate of Origin](https://developercertificate.org/). Everyone participating follows the [Code of Conduct](CODE_OF_CONDUCT.md).
